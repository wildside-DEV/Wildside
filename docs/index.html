How to track

  1. Walk the map. Tap where you want to go. Animals turn up where, when and in the season the real species lives.
  2. Creep in. Tap a marker inside your dashed circle. Step closer while the awareness bar is low, and freeze when the animal twitches.
  3. Search Dens. Teal bird boxes on the map hold kit. Walk up, swipe the disc, and come back in 5 minutes for more.
  4. Find the real thing. Each card tells you where to look in real life. Photograph a real one to earn a gold card.

Keep your distance from real wildlife. Use zoom, not your feet.
